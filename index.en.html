Member sharing

Compare supply nodes, or consume and supply idle member-session compute.

Worth examining

Supplier-set prices, site promotions, and public online Pelican tests and historical results. The sharing mechanism lets users participate in compute supply with usage-based settlement. Pelican records show a particular test; they do not establish model identity or general reliability.

Check before choosing

Multiple sources require screening. SESSION depends on both Agents and an online supplier. A public CODEX_SESSION query on 2026-10-09 returned zero listings; confirm current supply. Some pricing units need clarification. The platform temporarily retains recent requests and responses; suppliers may read member-session code. Public terms list default concurrency of one and no invoices.

OpenRouter ↗

Routing control

Express provider choices and data policies in request settings.

Worth examining

Select or exclude providers, configure fallbacks, and set price, latency or throughput preferences. Data-collection and ZDR filters help express data requirements.

Credit-purchase fees and BYOK rules affect the budget. Restricting providers or data policies narrows fallback choices. Latency and throughput preferences depend on available supply.

Uncached input and cache-read counts do not overlap. Enter the ordinary input price if cache reads are billed as cache misses. Quotes must include group multipliers and use a 1:1 cash-credit basis in the selected currency. Extra charges cover the entire workload and can include cache writes, tools and failed retries. Bonus credits are assumed eligible and fully used; top-up surcharges apply to purchased credits. Minimum top-ups, minimum fees, taxes, exchange costs and unentered charges are not automatically included. This estimator does not cover periodic subscriptions.

Calculations run in your browser. Parameters are included in links you choose to share; anyone receiving a link can read its quotes and workload. Exports are budget estimates, not billing or calling-test evidence.
